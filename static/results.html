1 · Does verification actually help?

safety +0.0+1.2-1.1   — the sign is not stable

Paired exact McNemar over all runs: safety b=2, c=2, p = 1.0000 · responsiveness b=19, c=3, p = 0.0009

Grounding is the whole effect. S0 → S1 moves safety from ~49% to ~97%. S1 → S2 is inside the noise on safety and costs ~6 points of responsiveness. The component stays for reasons argued in docs/RESULTS.md §1 — none of which is "the eval said so".

MUTE is the control that makes the point. A system answering everything with one fixed safe string is 100% safe and 59.6% responsive. Any safety number quoted without a responsiveness number beside it is achievable by saying nothing.

2 · Triage cascade

Same 189 held-out messages from two platforms, gate threshold 0.23, fit on 609 training rows never tested against.

armprecisionrecallF1tp/fp/fn
A0 · regex baseline (incumbent-style)77.3%45.9%57.6%17/5/20
A1 · logistic gate, 15 features50.0%89.2%64.1%33/33/4
A2 · full cascade (gate → model)87.5%75.7%81.2%28/4/9

A2 has a model in it, so it moves: precision 84.8–87.5%, false positives 5–4. A0 and A1 are deterministic.

The gate's job is recall, not precision. A1 at 50% precision looks bad alone; it is a filter feeding a model, and 33 false positives is the price of missing only 4. Stage 2 removes most of them.

Populations differ and it matters. On the 161-row single-platform segment A0 recall is 40.7%; pooled over all observed messages it is 53.6%. Same arm, different denominators.

What did not survive measurement

“verification makes the system safer” — not demonstrated; see §1.

“the cascade beats the incumbent on a new platform” — a wash, F1 79.4% vs 80.0%.

“the incumbent is unstable” — chi-square p = 0.130, and the test was never licensed (min expected cell 4.56 < 5).

“97.8% safe” as a headline — unfalsifiable; the MUTE row scores 100%.

“verification is a 0.2 ms dict lookup” — measured on one synthetic draft; it is 1.143 ms p95 CPU over the real corpus.

Suite E's 10/10, Suite C's 36/36 — 216 of 2,500 threshold pairs also score 10/10; C drops to 77% under realistic chat noise.
